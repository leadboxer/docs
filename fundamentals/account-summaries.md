# Account Summaries (AI)

An account summary is a short, AI-written overview of how an account's contacts and visitors interact with your website: who visited, which pages they looked at, and what that suggests about their interest. Use it to get up to speed on an account before you reach out.

***

### Generate a summary

1. Open **Reports** and go to the **Top accounts** table.
2. Click **Generate Summary** for an account.
3. The summary appears in the table once it is ready.

Click **What is this?** next to the button for a short explanation.

### Summarize all accounts

To generate summaries for every account in the current table at once, use **Summarize all**. A confirmation shows how many accounts will be summarized and what it will cost. Depending on the size of the table, it can take a few minutes to generate all summaries.

***

### Credits

Each account summary uses **100 credits**. See [LeadBoxer Credits](elements/leadboxer-credits.md) and [LeadBoxer Pricing Explained](leadboxer-pricing-explained.md).

### Tips

* Summaries are based on the visits and contacts LeadBoxer has collected for the account, so they are most useful for accounts with several visits.
* Generate a new summary after an account has been active again to include the latest visits.
