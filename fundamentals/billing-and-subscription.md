# Billing & Subscription

You manage your LeadBoxer plan, payment details and invoices on the **Billing** page. Open it from the profile menu (top right) and choose **Billing**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Don't see Billing in the menu? Your role may not include billing. Ask the owner of your LeadBoxer account.
{% endhint %}

***

### Choose or upgrade a plan

The Billing page shows the available plans and the credits each one includes. Pick a plan to start a subscription, or click **Upgrade Plan** to move to a bigger one.

Upgrades take effect immediately. For plan prices and what each plan includes, see [LeadBoxer Pricing Explained](leadboxer-pricing-explained.md).

### Update payment details and download invoices

Click **Update Payment Details** or **Download Invoices** on the Billing page. This opens the secure billing portal of our payment provider (Stripe), where you can:

* change your credit card or payment method
* update your billing address and VAT details
* download past invoices

### Change or cancel your subscription

1. Open **Billing** from the profile menu.
2. Click **Upgrade Plan** to open the billing portal.
3. In the billing portal, change your plan or cancel your subscription.

Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then you keep your plan and your included credits.

Can't find the option, or want help choosing a plan? Email us at [hello@leadboxer.com](mailto:hello@leadboxer.com).

***

### Credits and usage

Your plan includes a monthly number of credits. To see your balance and what used your credits, open **Credit Overview** from the profile menu. See [LeadBoxer Credits](elements/leadboxer-credits.md) for details.
