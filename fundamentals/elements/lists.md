# Lists



### Introduction: what are Lists?

A List (Account List) is a list of companies or organisations that you want LeadBoxer to recognise and do something with: for example your existing clients, former clients, or a list of target accounts for Account Based Marketing (ABM). If you need to filter on a large number of accounts, Lists are what you need.

The LeadBoxer Lists feature allows you to:&#x20;

1. Upload a list of company names and / or domain names.&#x20;
2. Apply this list as a Filter and make part of your Segments (and notifications)

### How Lists work

1. Go to **List Settings** and download the sample CSV file.
2. Add your accounts (domain and/or company name; wildcards are allowed) and upload the file.
3. In the filters, open **Lists** and select your list, then save the result as a [Segment](segments.md).
4. Use that segment for [notifications](notifications.md), LeadBoards or reports.

On the List Settings page you can also download, replace or remove a list you uploaded earlier.
