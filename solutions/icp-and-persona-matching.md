# ICP & persona matching

Not every company that visits your website or opens your emails is worth your time. ICP & persona matching lets you describe who you want to sell to once, and LeadBoxer App applies it to every lead from then on.

## What it does

* **Tags ICP matches.** Companies that fit your [Ideal Customer Profile](../fundamentals/definitions-and-glossary.md#icp-ideal-customer-profile) (industry, employee size, country) are tagged automatically as they engage with your website and emails.
* **Finds the right people.** For each matching company, LeadBoxer lists the people who fit your [personas](../fundamentals/definitions-and-glossary.md#persona) (job title, seniority, country), with their professional profile.
* **Shows matches where you work.** In [Inboxer](../fundamentals/inboxer.md), matched accounts and personas are highlighted, and you can filter on ICP and persona matches anywhere in the App.
* **Gets you a way in.** Where available, you can look up a persona's business email address for outreach.

## Get started

1. Define one or more ICP and persona combinations under [Goals & Targets](../fundamentals/elements/goals-and-targets.md#icp-and-personas). Not sure where to start? The free [ICP Generator](https://app.leadboxer.com/icp-generator) creates ICPs and personas from your company's domain name.
2. Open Inboxer or Leads & Accounts and filter on ICP matches.
3. Open a matching account to see its personas, and look up email addresses for the ones you want to contact.

Persona match lookups and persona email lookups use credits. See [LeadBoxer Credits](../fundamentals/elements/leadboxer-credits.md).
